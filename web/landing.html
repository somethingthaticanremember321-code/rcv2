Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers regarding app functionality, accuracy, and policies.

Are my receipt photos uploaded or stored on external servers?

No. DashTally uses a private-by-design architecture. Photos are processed directly on your smartphone using on-device ML Kit optical character recognition. Original images are kept strictly inside your phone's sandbox storage and are never uploaded or retained in the cloud.

How does DashTally handle folded or faded receipts?

Our spatial line sorting groups OCR text by physical vertical and horizontal coordinates, reconstructing columns correctly even if paper has been creased or stepped on. If a digit is faded, our 4x pinch-to-zoom screen allows you to inspect the number in two seconds.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

All purchases are managed directly by Apple or Google. On iPhone, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & Subscriptions. You can cancel at any time with 1 tap.

Does DashTally provide tax or legal accounting advice?

No. DashTally is a receipt capture and organization utility designed to create clean CSV spreadsheets. You are solely responsible for reviewing extracted numbers against original slips prior to tax filing or reimbursement submission.
