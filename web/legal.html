Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 • DashTally Version 1.0
Private-by-Design: DashTally processes receipt photos locally on your device via on-device ML Kit OCR. Your original receipt photos are never uploaded to or stored on external cloud servers.

1 Information We Do NOT Collect

DashTally does not collect:

  • Receipt Photos: Photos taken or imported stay strictly in your device sandbox. Photos never leave your phone.
  • Bank Credentials: We never request or access banking logins or payment credentials.
  • Precise Location: We do not collect GPS coordinates.

2 Data Processing & AI Structuring

To convert your receipts into clean spreadsheets:

  • On-Device OCR: Google ML Kit runs locally on your phone's processor to extract text.
  • Encrypted Structuring: Extracted text lines are sent over TLS 1.3 solely for JSON field formatting.
  • No AI Model Training: Under enterprise non-training commitments, your text is processed in memory and never used for model training.
  • Local Storage: Returned fields are stored exclusively in your local database.

3 Telemetry & Analytics

PostHog collects minimal anonymized diagnostics (app launches, scan counts, crash events). We never log receipt images, merchant names, line items, or financial amounts to analytics.

4 Data Ownership & Deletion

You can export your complete ledger to CSV at any time, or permanently erase all local records with one tap in Settings → Clear All Receipts.
