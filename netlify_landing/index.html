Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Ahl app, your privacy, and data security.

Does Ahl connect to my bank or store my passwords?

Never. Ahl is built on a 100% Local-First architecture. We never ask for, transmit, or store bank credentials, passwords, or account numbers. All your data lives securely inside your device’s local storage.

How does multi-contributor income work without mental math?

In your setup wizard or Settings, simply tap "Add Contributor". Enter names (e.g. Self, Spouse, Child) and their individual monthly salaries. Ahl automatically sums them and balances your total household cash flow in real-time.

How does the Subscription Radar work?

Ahl includes a pre-indexed directory of 117+ services popular in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman (Jahez, Hungerstation, Shahid, TOD, ChatGPT, etc.). You can search, toggle them on, or add custom services in seconds.

Can I use Ahl completely offline?

Yes. Ahl requires no internet connection for day-to-day budgeting, calculating expenses, or managing subscriptions. Everything runs locally on your smartphone.

What happens if I change my phone?

You can export a full database backup or an Excel/CSV spreadsheet directly from Settings. On your new phone, restore your backup with a single tap.
