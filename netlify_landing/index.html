Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Mali app, your privacy, and data security.

Does Mali connect to my bank or store my passwords?

Never. Mali is built on a 100% Local-First architecture. We never ask for, transmit, or store bank credentials, passwords, or account numbers. All your data lives securely inside your device’s local storage.

Can I use Mali as a single individual or is it only for families?

Mali is designed for both! Solo users can effortlessly track their primary income, personal subscriptions, and Zakat. If you share finances with a spouse or partner, you can optionally tap "Add Income Source" to sum inflows with zero mental math.

How does the Subscription Radar work?

Mali includes a pre-indexed directory of 117+ services popular in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, and Oman (Jahez, Hungerstation, Shahid, TOD, ChatGPT, etc.). You can search, toggle them on, or add custom services in seconds.

Can I use Mali completely offline?

Yes. Mali requires no internet connection for day-to-day budgeting, calculating expenses, or managing subscriptions. Everything runs locally on your smartphone.

What happens if I change my phone?

You can export a full database backup or an Excel/CSV spreadsheet directly from Settings. On your new phone, restore your backup with a single tap.

Can I try Mali Pro before paying?

Yes! Our Annual plan includes a full 7-Day Free Trial. You get complete access to every Pro feature risk-free. If you decide it's not for you, cancel anytime in Google Play before Day 7 and you won't be charged a single cent.
