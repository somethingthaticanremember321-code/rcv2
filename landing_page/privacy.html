Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026 • Version 1.0
Private-by-Design Architecture: DashTally processes receipt photos locally on your device using on-device optical character recognition (OCR). Your original receipt photos are never uploaded to, stored on, or viewed by external cloud servers.

1 Information We Do NOT Collect

DashTally is deliberately built to minimize data collection. We specifically do not collect or store:

  • Receipt Images / Photos: Receipt photos taken with your camera or imported from your photo library are saved strictly in your device's local sandbox storage. Image files never leave your device.
  • Banking & Payment Credentials: We never ask for, access, or store your bank accounts, credit card numbers, debit cards, or financial logins.
  • Precise Location Data: We do not track or store your physical GPS location.

2 Data Processing & AI Text Structuring

To convert your receipt text into organized columns (merchant name, transaction date, sales tax, total amount, and category):

  • Local OCR Extraction: Google ML Kit performs on-device text recognition directly on your phone's processor.
  • Secure Transmission of Text Lines: Only the extracted raw text characters are transmitted over an encrypted TLS 1.3 connection to a secure AI structuring API solely for JSON field parsing.
  • Zero Model Training: Under our enterprise privacy commitments, receipt text is processed in memory and is never stored, retained, or used to train artificial intelligence models.
  • Local Storage: Once structured fields are returned, they are saved exclusively into your local on-device database.

3 Product Telemetry & Analytics

We use PostHog to collect minimal, aggregated product telemetry to monitor application stability, crash diagnostics, and measure conversion funnels:

  • What We Track: App installation events, scan attempt counts, feature usage taps, and paywall conversions.
  • What We NEVER Track: We never log, transmit, or attach receipt image files, merchant names, individual line items, or financial dollar amounts to analytics events.

4 Purchases & In-App Subscriptions

All in-app purchases, annual subscriptions, and Lifetime Pro licenses are processed directly through the official Google Play Billing system (Android) or Apple App Store In-App Purchases (iOS). DashTally does not collect or store credit card numbers. Purchase entitlement verification is managed securely via RevenueCat using anonymous user identifiers.

5 Data Ownership, Export & Deletion

You retain 100% ownership of your business and financial records:

  • Instant CSV Export: You can export your entire ledger to a clean CSV file compatible with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, QuickBooks, or Numbers at any time.
  • 1-Tap Permanent Deletion: You can delete individual receipts or purge your entire database at any time by navigating to Settings → Clear All Receipts. Because records are stored locally, clearing your data permanently removes it from your phone immediately.

6 Third-Party Services

DashTally interacts with the following service providers solely to provide core application functionality:

  • Google ML Kit: On-device text recognition library (runs locally on CPU/NPU).
  • RevenueCat: In-app purchase verification and subscription entitlement management.
  • PostHog: Anonymized product analytics and diagnostic telemetry.
